# Final-Year Project Documentation Outline

## 1. Title
SmartCampus AI: An AI-Based College Management and Student Academic Assistance System

## 2. Abstract
A centralized web-based platform for managing college academic information, schedules, notices, attendance, examinations, assignments, and student support. AI/ML components provide academic-support predictions and a college-specific assistant.

## 3. Problem Statement
Traditional college information is often distributed across notices, spreadsheets, messaging applications, and separate systems. Students and faculty need a centralized platform for academic information and management.

## 4. Objectives
- Centralize academic information
- Provide role-based access
- Simplify attendance and marks management
- Provide timetable and notice access
- Add AI/ML-based academic analytics
- Provide an academic assistant

## 5. Modules
- Authentication
- Student management
- Faculty management
- Academics
- Timetable
- Attendance
- Examinations
- Assignments
- Notices
- AI/ML prediction
- AI assistant
- Reports

## 6. Future Scope
- Mobile application
- Face-based attendance with appropriate consent and safeguards
- Parent portal
- Placement management
- Library integration
- Hostel management
- Transport management
- Advanced analytics
